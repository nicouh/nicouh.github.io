# Privacy Policy for Trinkbrunnen und Toiletten

_Last updated: March 2026_

Thank you for using **Trinkbrunnen und Toiletten** (referred to in this document as "the App", "we", "our", or "us").  
This Privacy Policy explains in detail what kind of information is collected, how it may be used, and what rights you have.

---

## 1. General Principles

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.  
The App helps you find drinking fountains and public toilets in your area. It does **not** require an account and does not store personal information like names, email addresses, or precise location history on any servers.

However, the App does collect limited **diagnostic and usage data** to understand how the app is performing and to deliver advertisements. We also use third-party services that may collect data automatically.

---

## 2. Information We Collect Locally (On Your Device Only)

The App stores the following information **only on your device**:
- Your favorite fountains and toilets
- Recent searches
- UI preferences (language, dark mode, map style, routing provider)
- Downloaded fountain and toilet datasets (cached for offline use)

This data is **NOT sent to any servers** and remains private to your device.

---

## 3. Diagnostic Data We Send to Our Servers

On the first launch of the App, and periodically thereafter, we send the following **anonymous diagnostic data** to our backend (powered by Supabase) to monitor app performance and stability:

- App version number
- Package name (app identifier)
- Device model (e.g., "iPhone 14", "Samsung Galaxy S23")
- Operating system version (e.g., "iOS 17", "Android 14")
- Country (derived from device settings)
- Approximate city (derived from device settings, not precise GPS)
- Number of app startups/launches

**Why we collect this:**
- To understand which devices and OS versions work well with the App
- To identify crashes or performance issues across different platforms
- To understand the geographic distribution of our users

**What we do NOT collect:**
- Your precise location
- Any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
- Your browsing history or fountain/toilet searches
- App usage patterns or feature interactions

This data is automatically deleted after a reasonable retention period. It is not shared with third parties except where necessary for technical operations.

---

## 4. Information Collected by Third Parties

The App uses services provided by third parties that may automatically collect certain data:

### Google AdMob (Advertisements)
The App displays advertisements via **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and process data automatically, including but not limited to:
- Device advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA)
- Approximate location (country/region) for ad relevance
- App usage data (e.g., number of impressions)
- Interactions with ads (e.g., clicks)

For more details, please review Google's privacy policies:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Advertising and Marketing Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [AdMob Help Center](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

### Location and Mapping Services
When you use location-based features or search, the App may communicate with:
- **OpenStreetMap Nominatim** for address search and geocoding
- **OpenStreetMap, Carto, or other tile providers** for map display
- Your device's location services (if permission is granted)

These services may receive your IP address and, if location permission is enabled, approximate or precise coordinates needed to fulfill your request.

### External Routing Applications
When you choose to open directions in an external app (Google Maps, Apple Maps, Bing Maps, OpenStreetMap, etc.), the coordinates of the fountain/toilet are shared with that app. You should review the privacy policies of those apps.

---

## 5. Purpose of Data Use

**Diagnostic data (sent to our Supabase backend)** is used for:
- Monitoring app stability and performance
- Identifying bugs or crashes
- Understanding user demographics for app improvement
- Ensuring the app works across various devices and OS versions

**Data collected by Google AdMob** is used for:
- Delivering personalized or non-personalized ads
- Measuring advertising effectiveness
- Preventing fraud and ensuring security of the advertising network
- Improving Google's services

**Location data (on your device and when you search)** is used for:
- Showing nearby fountains and toilets
- Calculating distances and sorting by proximity
- Geocoding addresses you search for
- Providing map functionality

---

## 6. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

You can choose to disable personalized ads at any time:

**On Android:**
- Go to **Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account**
- Tap **Data & Privacy**
- Scroll to **Ad settings** and tap **Manage your Google Ads settings**
- Tap **Personalization** and toggle it **off**

**On iOS:**
- Go to **Settings → Privacy → Apple Advertising**
- Tap **Personalized Ads** and toggle it **off**

You can also reset your advertising ID in your device settings, which will prevent future ads from being linked to your previous behavior.

---

## 7. Location Permissions

The App may request location permission to:
- Show nearby fountains and toilets
- Sort results by distance
- Center the map on your current location

**You can grant or deny this permission at any time.** If you deny location permission, the App will still work, but distance-based features will be limited. You can search by address instead.

---

## 8. Data Security

- **Diagnostic data** sent to our backend is transmitted securely over HTTPS.
- **Local data** on your device is as secure as your device's own security measures.
- We do not store passwords, payment information, or sensitive personal data.
- Data handled by third parties (Google, OpenStreetMap, etc.) is managed according to their own privacy and security policies.

---

## 9. Children's Privacy

The App is **not directed at children under the age of 13** in the EU, or under applicable age limits in your jurisdiction.  
If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through third-party services such as Google AdMob, please contact Google directly.

---

## 10. Offline Mode and Data Retention

The App can function in offline mode using locally cached data. You can:
- Clear all app data by going to **Device Settings → Apps → Trinkbrunnen und Toiletten → Storage → Clear Data**
- Delete the App entirely to remove all locally stored data
- Request that we delete your diagnostic data by contacting us (see Section 12)
